## 2. Bug Fixed

Duplicate `mmc5Ref.NotifyVramRead(0x2000 | (vram_addr & 0x0FFF))` at dots 337/339 — called once inside the `evalDot >= 337` block AND once unconditionally outside. MMC5 games would receive double VRAM read notifications at these dots, potentially causing incorrect scanline counter behavior.
